# Hi, I'm Mahdi 👋

**Product leader who still ships code** · Tehran, Iran

I lead customer-side product at **Digikala**, Iran's largest e-commerce platform. My area covers discovery, engagement and what happens after purchase. I started out as a backend and AI engineer, so I'm as comfortable reading a diff or tuning a prompt as I am arguing about a metric.

Most of the code here comes from small tools I built for my own problems: in product work, in reading, and on my 3D printer.
